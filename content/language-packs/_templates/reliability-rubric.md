# Reliability rubric (1–5)

Score each criterion 1–5. Overall = rounded average, but overall can never be
more than one point above the lowest criterion (one weak link caps the pack).

| Criterion | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Source quality** | Critical edition or the object itself (photo/scan, museum record) | Reputable published translation without full apparatus | Popular, anonymous, or untraceable source |
| **Text condition** | Complete, clearly legible | Some breaks, restorations widely accepted | Heavily broken or mostly restored |
| **Translation confidence** | Standard vocabulary, grammar clear, lexicons agree | Some rare words or ambiguous grammar | Many hapax/unknown words, reading is conjectural |
| **Scholarly consensus** | Specialists agree on the reading | Live debate with a majority view | Contested or fringe-only reading |
| **Citation completeness** | Every rendering tied to edition + lexicon, links given | Most claims cited | Few or no citations |
| **License / legal status** | Clear PD, CC0, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA; terms checked; ready for a pack PDF | Mixed, share-alike obligations not yet mapped, or permission requested but not received | Copyrighted, CC-BY-NC, unknown, or otherwise not cleared — `reference-only`, no PDF |
| **Witness date / distance** | Autograph, or a photograph/edition of a witness close in time to composition | Normal medieval (or later) manuscript tradition of an ancient work, apparatus available | Known mainly from much later quotation, a modern reconstruction, or no witness apparatus |

Labels: 5 solid, 4 good, 3 usable with caveats, 2 weak (flag on camera),
1 do not present as fact.

Source tiers for sources.md: PRIMARY > SECONDARY (peer-reviewed / university
press) > SECONDARY (popular) > online summary. Occult-community and modern
devotional sources are logged honestly as such: useful for practice context,
never as evidence for what an ancient text says.

## License field and PDFs (added 2026-11-10)

Every pack's front matter has a `license` line: `PD`, `CC-BY`, `CC-BY-SA`, or
`reference-only`. Only the first three may be rendered to PDF. Copyrighted
modern editions and translations (for example Betz's *PGM* translation, the
Robinson/Meyer/Layton Nag Hammadi translations, Athanassakis's Orphic Hymns)
are logged as **REFERENCE-ONLY**. They may be cited in `sources.md` and used
to check a reading; their wording must not be copied into a pack PDF.

CC-BY-NC is treated as `reference-only` for PDF purposes unless and until the
archive's distribution model is confirmed to be non-commercial and the
license terms are met. When in doubt, do not put it in a PDF.

Jurisdiction matters. A work may be public domain in the United States and
still in copyright in the EU/UK (life + 70). Packs note both when they
differ. The working default for this archive, whose user is in the US, is US
status, with the other status flagged ⚠️ where it would block reuse abroad.

## Witness date / distance (added 2026-11-10)

This criterion is for the PDF user as much as for the pack: how far is the
surviving witness from the date of composition? A first-century letter
surviving in a fourth-century codex (for example Codex Sinaiticus for the
Greek NT) is a different kind of evidence from a Sumerian poem on an Old
Babylonian tablet, and both are different from a late antique hymn known
only from a Renaissance printed edition. State the date of the work, the
date of the witness actually used, and the gap, in the pack's source-text
section.
