# Intake: Jacqueline’s own books and grimoires

This folder is a **register**, not a scrape target and not a pack
container. Use it when Jacqueline already owns a physical or digital
copy that should be logged for the archive.

Packs still live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`. A completed
intake form may later be linked from a case-study `sources.md`. Nothing
here is marked READY, translated, or turned into a PDF until the
copyright status is clear.

## What is allowed

- **Private research and reference.** An owned copy (print or legally
  purchased ebook) may be read, cited, and used as a REFERENCE-ONLY
  comparandum in a pack. Ownership is not a publication licence.
- **Reproduction and pack PDFs.** Only works that are public domain,
  CC0, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA (attribution and share-alike honored), or
  that carry a **written licence** allowing this archive to reproduce
  them, may be copied into a pack, quoted at length, or rendered as a
  PDF. CC-BY-NC stays reference-only unless distribution is confirmed
  non-commercial.

## What is not allowed

- Scanning or retyping a still-copyrighted grimoire, modern edition, or
  translation into a pack PDF just because the book is on the shelf.
- Treating a personal inscription, family copy, or “out of print”
  status as a rights clearance.
- Spell-work or ritual how-to extracted from an owned manual for
  publication. The center is historical and research-only.

## How to log an item

1. Copy `intake-template.md` to a new file in this folder, named
   after a short slug (for example `intake-grand-grimoire-1900.md`).
2. Fill in title, edition/year, publisher, translator, and copyright
   status. Leave no copyright field blank; use `check` if unverified.
3. Set the recommended use: `private-reference-only`, `PDF-OK`, or
   `needs-rights-review`.
4. Do not start a pack or a PDF until a second check agrees with that
   status.
