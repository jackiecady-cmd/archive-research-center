# Sources: Satan and Lucifer case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

Each entry is **tagged** for the figure it actually refers to
(adversary lineage / morning-star lineage / conflation), per the style
rule in `README.md`.

## Adversary lineage

1. **[PRIMARY] [adversary lineage]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible — Job 1–2; Zechariah 3; Numbers 22:22; 1 Chronicles 21:1; 2 Samuel 24:1 (the last is the parallel in which *śāṭān* is *not* used). https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb — WLC text PD; morphology CC BY 4.0 — PDF: PDF-OK (credit OSHB if morphology is used) — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY] [adversary lineage]** SBL Greek New Testament (Holmes, 2010), CC BY 4.0 — Σατανᾶς and διάβολος. Not 2 Peter 1:19 (that verse is morning-star vocabulary; see below) — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY] [adversary lineage]** Septuagint — διάβολος where it renders *śāṭān* / *ha-satan*. Named PD Greek (e.g. Swete if confirmed PD); Brenton English US PD if used. Rahlfs and later editions often REFERENCE-ONLY — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY] [adversary-related, not morning-star]** *Jubilees* (Mastema) — R. H. Charles, 1913 *APOT* or *The Book of Jubilees*, US PD if that printing is used — PDF: PDF-OK for Charles 1913 — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY] [adversary-related, not morning-star]** Qumran War Scroll and related Belial texts — Leon Levy DSS Digital Library images typically LINK-ONLY; modern editions (DJD, etc.) **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[REFERENCE-ONLY until a zoroastrian pack is opened] [comparative claim about the adversary lineage, not about *hēlēl*]** Avestan / Pahlavi texts on Angra Mainyu / Ahriman — see the source catalog (Avesta.org and TITUS pages mixed; modern editions often in copyright)

## Morning-star lineage

7. **[PRIMARY] [morning-star lineage]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / OSHB — Isaiah 14:12 *hēlēl ben-šāḥar* only. Same licence as item 1. This verse does not use *śāṭān* — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[PRIMARY] [morning-star lineage]** Septuagint, Isaiah 14:12 ἑωσφόρος — same PD-edition rule as item 3 — candidate, not yet reviewed
9. **[PRIMARY] [morning-star lineage]** SBLGNT, 2 Peter 1:19 φωσφόρος (*phōsphoros*) — Christ as morning star; not Σατανᾶς — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
10. **[PRIMARY] [morning-star lineage]** Latin Vulgate — Isaiah 14:12 *lucifer*; 2 Peter 1:19 *lucifer*. Clementine or another pre-1929 printing is US PD; the Stuttgart / Weber edition is typically in copyright — **check** the named text — PDF: PDF-OK for a confirmed-PD printing. The *word* here is morning-star language; Jerome’s later *identification* of Isaiah 14 with the adversary is conflation (item 12) — candidate, not yet reviewed
11. **[SECONDARY] [morning-star parallels; ⚠️ UNCERTAIN if equated too tightly]** Ugaritic Šaḥar / Athtar — modern corpora often in copyright, **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PDF-OK text is named — candidate, not yet reviewed

## Conflation

13. **[PRIMARY] [conflation]** John Milton, *Paradise Lost* (1667/1674) — English US PD. Literary working-out of the already conflated figure — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
14. **[PRIMARY] [tag by the name each text uses; conflation where an author treats the two as one]** William Blake, Percy Bysshe Shelley, Lord Byron — named PD texts (pre-1929 printings) — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
