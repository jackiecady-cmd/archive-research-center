# Case study: Judaism (texts and traditions)

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

A historical and linguistic dossier on **Jewish primary texts** and later
interpretive literature, not a confessional handbook and not a duplicate
of [`../yeshua/`](../yeshua/). Lilith and *shedim* links go also to
[`../demonization/`](../demonization/) and
[`../lilith/`](../lilith/) (first pack `sumerian_lilith`, which includes
Isaiah 34:14, Bavli notices, and bowls as layer-2 overview).
The center is non-biased: faith claims are recorded as claims of a
source or tradition. Historical only; no practical Kabbalah instruction.

## Scope

- **Tanakh** as a linguistic base (WLC / OSHB), used here for passages
  that are not already owned by another case study, or cross-linked
  when they are.
- **Targums** (Aramaic), from a named, rights-cleared text.
- **Second Temple and midrashic** material that is PDF-OK (for example
  the Alphabet of Ben Sira, if a Sefaria or PD text’s licence allows).
- **Sefer Yetzirah** and other early kabbalistic or proto-kabbalistic
  texts, from named PD Hebrew printings and, if used, a dated PD
  English (e.g. Westcott, with a quality warning). The *Zohar* and most
  modern Kabbalah translations are typically still in copyright —
  REFERENCE-ONLY until a cleared edition is named.
- **Talmud and midrash** via Sefaria or named PD printings; **licence
  is per text**. Soncino and most 20th-c. English is REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Dead Sea Scrolls** as Jewish textual context (already noted under
  Yeshua as not Christian documents), including the **Songs of the Sage**
  (4Q510–511). Images usually LINK-ONLY; DJD and modern English
  REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Hekhalot / Merkavah** literature (including *Hekhalot Rabbati*),
  from a named, rights-cleared Hebrew text. Modern synopses (e.g.
  Schäfer) are typically REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Babylonian Aramaic incantation bowls** that name Lilith and other
  demons; cross-link [`../demonization/`](../demonization/). Montgomery
  1913 may be US PD; later corpora (Naveh/Shaked, Levene, and others)
  REFERENCE-ONLY; object images usually LINK-ONLY.
- **Sefer Raziel** (*Sefer Raziel HaMalakh*): early-modern printings may
  be PD (**check** the title); modern translations REFERENCE-ONLY. No
  practical-magic instruction.
- **Talmud demonology** (Ashmedai, *shedim*, and related notices),
  linked to [`../demonization/`](../demonization/). Hebrew/Aramaic from
  a named PD printing if used; Soncino and most modern English
  REFERENCE-ONLY.

Sefaria and similar platforms are finding aids: **per-text licence**,
never a blanket PDF-OK.

## Method

- Date the witness (biblical, tannaitic, medieval, early-modern print).
- Do not read later Kabbalah back into Genesis or into Job.
- Academic and traditional readings are labelled as such.
