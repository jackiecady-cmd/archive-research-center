# Sources: Yeshua case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible — https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb — WLC text PD; morphology CC BY 4.0 — PDF: PDF-OK (credit OSHB if morphology is used) — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** SBL Greek New Testament (Holmes, 2010), CC BY 4.0 — https://sblgnt.com/license/ — https://github.com/Faithlife/SBLGNT — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Codex Sinaiticus (4th c. witness) — https://www.codexsinaiticus.org/ (TLS not confirmed from this environment 2026-11-10) — transcription/image terms not re-verified — PDF: not cleared for project images; can be cited as a witness beside SBLGNT — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Sahidic Coptic NT, Coptic Scriptorium corpora — https://github.com/CopticScriptorium/corpora — licence **per document**; Sahidica files may be special-licensed — PDF: not cleared until the document header is read — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Gospel of Thomas, Nag Hammadi Codex II (Coptic text) — the ancient wording is not in copyright; photographs and modern editions/translations (Robinson *NHL*, Layton, Meyer, Lambdin, etc.) are in copyright — PDF: modern English **REFERENCE-ONLY**; Coptic only if a rights-cleared transcription is used — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Peshitta (Syriac), older print editions and Murdock 1851 English (US PD) — locate a named edition when the pack is opened. Lamsa translation: **REFERENCE-ONLY**, not for PDFs — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[PRIMARY]** Josephus, *Antiquities* 18.63–64 and 20.200 — Greek: name a PD critical text (e.g. Niese, if confirmed PD); English: Whiston is US PD. The *Testimonium Flavianum* is textually disputed — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[PRIMARY]** Tacitus, *Annals* 15.44 — Latin from a named PD edition; English Church/Brodribb is US PD — candidate, not yet reviewed
9. **[PRIMARY]** Pliny the Younger, *Epistles* 10.96–97 — Latin from a named PD edition; older English translations exist in PD — candidate, not yet reviewed
10. **[SECONDARY]** Dead Sea Scrolls (Leon Levy DSS Digital Library and editions) — context only, not Jesus texts. Image licences are institutional; modern English editions (Wise, Vermes, etc.) are often in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PD/CC text is identified — candidate, not yet reviewed
11. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Recent commercial Bible translations (NIV, NRSV, etc.) — not for PDFs
12. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern Nag Hammadi English translations (Robinson, Meyer, Layton, Pagels’ popular presentations) — not for PDFs
