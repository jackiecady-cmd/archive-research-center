# Case study: Gnostic and related traditions

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

Historical and linguistic only. **No ritual instruction.** “Gnostic” is a
modern scholarly umbrella; do not collapse Sethian, Valentinian,
Mandaean, and heresiological reports into one system. Linked from the
pantheons index, and from [`../yeshua/`](../yeshua/) (Gospel of Thomas),
[`../mary-magdalene/`](../mary-magdalene/) (shared packs:
`coptic_gospel_mary`, `coptic_gospel_philip_magdalene`,
`coptic_pistis_sophia`, `coptic_dialogue_savior`, `coptic_gospel_thomas`),
and [`../satan-lucifer/`](../satan-lucifer/) where a text actually names
an adversary or a light-bearer figure — tagged, never interchangeably.
Shared packs are one top-level folder each; do not duplicate them.

## Scope

- **Nag Hammadi** and related Coptic literature: the Gospel of Thomas
  (Codex II), the Apocryphon of John, the Gospel of Philip, and other
  Sethian or Valentinian tractates as packs are opened. The fourth-
  century Coptic *wording* is not under copyright; photographs and
  modern critical editions and English translations (Robinson *NHL*,
  Layton, Meyer, Lambdin, etc.) **are**, and remain REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Pistis Sophia** and related Coptic-Askew / Bruce-codex literature,
  from a named PD edition if one exists (e.g. Mead 1896 English, with a
  quality warning) and a rights-cleared Coptic text.
- **Mandaean / Mandaic** literature, beginning with the *Ginza Rba*,
  kept as its own living and historical tradition, not as a footnote to
  Nag Hammadi.
- **Gospel of Judas**, Codex Tchacos. The Coptic wording is not under
  copyright; the National Geographic edition and translation (2006 and
  later) are **REFERENCE-ONLY**. Photographs follow the holders’ terms
  (usually LINK-ONLY).
- **Gospel of Mary**, Berlin Codex 8502 (Papyrus Berolinensis 8502),
  which also holds other tractates, plus the Greek fragments P.Oxy.
  3525 and P.Ryl. 463. Same pattern: Coptic and Greek wording not in
  copyright; modern editions and English REFERENCE-ONLY until a
  rights-cleared transcription is named. The pack `coptic_gospel_mary`
  is **shared** with [`../mary-magdalene/`](../mary-magdalene/).
- **Gospel of Philip**, Nag Hammadi Codex II,3, and the **Dialogue of
  the Savior**, NHC III,5, also listed from
  [`../mary-magdalene/`](../mary-magdalene/). In Philip, *koinōnos* and
  the lacuna at 63:34–36 are ⚠️ UNCERTAIN and must not be stated as a
  marriage claim.
- **Manichaean fragments** (Turfan, Medinet Madi, and related), as a
  related dualist literature, not as “Nag Hammadi.” Licence per edition;
  many modern corpora are REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Patristic reports** of groups later called gnostic (Irenaeus,
  **Hippolytus**, **Epiphanius**, and others) as *reports*, using ANF,
  PG, or other PD English/Greek/Latin, not as if they were the
  communities’ own texts. Modern translations of Epiphanius (e.g.
  Williams) are REFERENCE-ONLY.

The “Gospel of Jesus’ Wife” is a **forgery** and is recorded under
[`../mary-magdalene/`](../mary-magdalene/), not as a primary pack here.

Out of scope until separately commissioned: modern ritual “gnostic
mass” material and copyrighted contemporary commentaries used as if
they were primary.

## Method

- Date the Coptic witness, the putative Greek original (when argued),
  and the modern edition separately.
- Academic disagreement about “Gnosticism” as a category is flagged,
  not resolved by preference.
