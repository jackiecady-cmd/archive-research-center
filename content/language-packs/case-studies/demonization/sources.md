# Sources: Demonization case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

## A. Philology and primary religious texts

1. **[PRIMARY]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible — https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb — 2 Kings 1 (Baal Zebub); Deuteronomy 32:17; Psalm 106:37; Psalm 96:5 MT (for comparison with the LXX); Ashtoreth, Molech, Dagon, Isaiah 34:14 as relevant. WLC text PD; morphology CC BY 4.0 — PDF: PDF-OK (credit OSHB if morphology is used) — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** SBL Greek New Testament (Holmes, 2010), CC BY 4.0 — https://sblgnt.com/license/ — Mark 3:22; Matthew 12:24; 1 Corinthians 10:20 — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Septuagint, Psalm 95(96):5 and related — name a PD Greek (e.g. Swete, late 19th c., if confirmed PD) and, if used, Brenton’s English (US PD). Rahlfs and later critical editions are often still in copyright — **check** the exact printing — PDF: PDF-OK only for a confirmed-PD edition — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Ugaritic and Phoenician Astarte / Ashtart texts — licence **per edition**. Many modern corpora (KTU/CAT and later) are in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PDF-OK text is named — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY / CONTEXT]** Akkadian *šēdu*, *lilitu*, Pazuzu, Lamashtu — CDLI (https://cdli.earth/, text generally reusable with attribution; images often NC / permission) and ORACC (CC BY-SA 3.0). ETCSL is **REFERENCE-ONLY** (editors’ copyright) and must not go into PDFs — candidate, not yet reviewed

## B. Later grimoire and encyclopedia reception (not evidence for section A)

6. **[PRIMARY, reception]** Johann Weyer, *Pseudomonarchia Daemonum* (1577) — Latin US PD. Older English translations: confirm edition and date — PDF: PDF-OK for the 1577 Latin and for a confirmed-PD English — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[PRIMARY, reception]** *Grand Grimoire* / *Le Dragon Rouge* — dating **uncertain** (claimed 1521, probably 18th–19th c.). A pre-1929 French printing may be US PD; modern annotated editions are **REFERENCE-ONLY**. Historical/textual use only; do not pack evocation instructions as a how-to — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[PRIMARY, reception]** Collin de Plancy, *Dictionnaire Infernal* — 1818 and especially the 1863 illustrated edition are US PD — PDF: PDF-OK for a named PD printing; later reprints with new matter **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
9. **[SECONDARY, reception]** Ars Goetia / *Lesser Key of Solomon* MSS and the 1904 Mathers/Crowley edition — see `../goetia/sources.md`. Cross-reference only here — candidate, not yet reviewed

10. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern commentaries, recent Bible translations, and contemporary occult manuals — cite, do not copy into a pack PDF. No spell-work instruction.
