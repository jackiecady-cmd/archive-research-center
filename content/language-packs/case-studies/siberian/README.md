# Case study: Siberian and Inner Asian traditions

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

Part of the pantheons index as a regional dossier, not as a single
“Siberian pantheon.” Historical, linguistic, and ethnographic. **No
ritual instruction.**

## Scope

- **Evenki** (and the wider Tungusic record), including the origin of
  the scholarly word *shaman* (Evenki *samān* / related forms). The
  loanword’s history is not a licence to treat all Siberian traditions
  as one system.
- **Sakha** (Yakut), including the **Olonkho** epic.
- **Buryat**, **Khanty** and **Mansi**, and **Nenets**, as separate
  linguistic and cultural records, not as interchangeable “shamanic
  tribes.”
- **Tengrism** and the **Old Turkic Orkhon inscriptions** (and related
  runiform texts), as a documentary Inner Asian layer distinct from
  later ethnographic description.
- **Ethnographic sources:** the Jesup North Pacific Expedition volumes
  of Waldemar (Vladimir) Bogoras and Waldemar Jochelson, many of them
  early-1900s and often US PD; S. M. Shirokogoroff (licence and date
  **check**; *Psychomental Complex of the Tungus*, 1935, is not
  automatically PD). **Mircea Eliade** (*Shamanism*, and related) is
  **REFERENCE-ONLY**.

**Required flags.** These are living traditions as well as historical
subjects. Ethnographers wrote from their own time, method, and often
colonial or missionary position; **bias must be flagged**, not smoothed
out. Packs should be cautious about sacred knowledge, names, and
practices that communities may not want republished as curiosities.
Older PD ethnography is still a witness to the ethnographer, not an
unfiltered “native text.”

## Method

- Keep epic (Olonkho), inscription (Orkhon), and ethnography in separate
  notes.
- Do not let Eliade’s comparative “shamanism” set the structure of the
  dossier; he is a modern interpreter, in copyright, and much criticised.
- No how-to, no “core shamanism” instruction, no harvesting of living
  ritual for a pack PDF.
