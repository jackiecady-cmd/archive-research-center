# Sources: Siberian and Inner Asian case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. PDF flags follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or
CC-BY-SA go into PDFs. Living-tradition sensitivity applies even when a
text is legally PD.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Old Turkic Orkhon (and related) inscriptions — 19th- and early-20th-c. editions by Vilhelm Thomsen and V. V. Radloff are typically US PD (**confirm** the printing). Later standard editions and translations (Talat Tekin and after) are often **REFERENCE-ONLY** — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Sakha Olonkho — named Sakha edition and any PD or openly licensed translation. Many 20th- and 21st-c. recensions and UNESCO-era publications are in copyright — **check**. Use only a cleared text; quote living recitation practice with care — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Waldemar Bogoras (Vladimir Bogoraz), Jesup North Pacific Expedition volumes (e.g. *The Chukchee*, early 1900s) — often US PD — PDF: PDF-OK after a title-level date check. Flag bias and method — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Waldemar Jochelson, Jesup volumes (Koryak, Yukaghir, and related, early 1900s) — often US PD, same caveats — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[SECONDARY, check]** S. M. Shirokogoroff, *Psychomental Complex of the Tungus* (1935) and related work — not a pre-1929 automatic US-PD title; renewal and jurisdiction **check**. Until cleared: **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Mircea Eliade, *Shamanism: Archaic Techniques of Ecstasy*, and later popular “core shamanism” literature — in copyright; comparative secondary literature, not a Siberian primary source
7. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Recent ethnographies and community publications unless a specific open licence is named. Do not scrape living ritual.
