# Case study: Antichrist

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

## Scope

A non-biased historical and linguistic dossier on the word ἀντίχριστος and
on related figures and readings that later tradition groups under that
name. The New Testament itself does not use the word in 2 Thessalonians or
in Revelation; those texts are in scope as related passages, not as hidden
occurrences of the term.

In scope:

- ἀντίχριστος in 1 John 2:18, 2:22, 4:3 and 2 John 7 (plural and singular
  uses, and the denial-formula in the epistles).
- 2 Thessalonians 2:3–4, the “man of lawlessness” / “man of sin” (ὁ ἄνθρωπος
  τῆς ἀνομίας / related textual variants), which does not use ἀντίχριστος.
- Revelation 13 (the beast) and related chapters: Revelation does **not**
  use the word ἀντίχριστος. That fact stays on the page.
- Daniel 7–11 as background often cited in this dossier (beasts, horns,
  the “little horn,” the desecration theme), kept labelled as earlier
  Hebrew/Aramaic context, not as a New Testament rewrite.
- Patristic development: Irenaeus, *Adversus Haereses* V.25–30; Hippolytus,
  *De Christo et Antichristo* (*On Christ and the Antichrist*).
- Islamic al-Masīḥ ad-Dajjāl in hadith, flagged as a **parallel tradition**,
  not as a source for, or a fulfilment of, the Johannine term.

A planned pack on **Revelation as coded political critique under Roman
persecution** (`greek_revelation_coded_rome`) is included because that is
likely what Jacqueline means; **confirm pending**. That pack covers, as
historical-critical options rather than as settled facts: the 666 / 616
gematria as *Nerōn Qesar* in Hebrew letters (Rev 13:18); Papyrus 115
(P.Oxy. LVI 4499) reading 616, and Irenaeus, *Adv. Haer.* V.30, already
noting copies that read 616; “Babylon” as Rome (Rev 17–18; cf. 1 Peter
5:13); the seven hills (Rev 17:9); the Nero *redivivus* legend (Tacitus,
*Histories* II.8; Suetonius, *Nero* 57; *Sibylline Oracles* 4–5); the
Domitian versus Nero dating debate; and persecution context (Tacitus,
*Annals* XV.44; Pliny, *Epistles* X.96–97), with the explicit note that
this persecution evidence is **debated by scholars**.

**Neutral split:** the preterist / historical-critical reading (including
the Rome-coded reading above) and futurist / religious readings are both
reported as readings. The pack does not choose a church’s eschatology, and
it does not treat the historical-critical reconstruction as a faith claim.

Out of scope until separately commissioned: systematic end-times theology,
confessional apologetics or polemic, and contemporary political
identifications of a living person as “the Antichrist.”

**Audience context only:** Peter Thiel has given public lectures on the
Antichrist. That is noted so the case study can serve readers who arrive
from that discussion. This file adds **no claims about him**, and no pack
treats those lectures as a source for the ancient texts.

## Method

- Academic consensus, minority scholarly positions, and faith claims are
  labelled as such. A religious reading is recorded as a reading, not as
  what “the text really says” to the exclusion of others.
- Every pack names its edition, licence, and the date of the witness.
- Copyrighted modern editions and translations are REFERENCE-ONLY and will
  not appear in PDFs. P.Oxy. images are LINK-ONLY.
