# Sources: Antichrist case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** SBL Greek New Testament (Holmes, 2010), CC BY 4.0 — https://sblgnt.com/license/ — https://github.com/Faithlife/SBLGNT — 1 John 2:18, 2:22, 4:3; 2 John 7; 2 Thessalonians 2:3–4; Revelation 13 and 17–18; 1 Peter 5:13 — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible — https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb — Daniel 7–11 (and the Aramaic of Daniel 4 if cross-referenced). WLC text PD; morphology CC BY 4.0 — PDF: PDF-OK (credit OSHB if morphology is used) — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Ante-Nicene Fathers (Roberts–Donaldson et al.), US PD — Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* V.25–30 (including V.30 on the 666 / 616 variation); Hippolytus, *On Christ and the Antichrist*. CCEL / Internet Archive, per-work licence check — PDF: PDF-OK for the ANF English; name a PD Greek/Latin if used — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Papyrus 115 (P.Oxy. LVI 4499), reading 616 at Rev 13:18 — images via the Oxyrhynchus / CSAD / papyri.info record: **LINK-ONLY**. The 1999 *Oxyrhynchus Papyri* volume is in copyright (**REFERENCE-ONLY**). The 616 reading may be reported and compared with Irenaeus; do not paste the modern edition — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Tacitus, *Annals* XV.44 and *Histories* II.8 — Latin from a named PD edition; English Church and Brodribb is US PD. A pre-1930 Loeb, if confirmed US PD for the volume used, may be cited; later Loebs are REFERENCE-ONLY — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Suetonius, *Nero* 57 — J. C. Rolfe, Loeb 1914, US PD, if that is the volume used; otherwise a named PD Latin and older English — PDF: PDF-OK for the 1914 Loeb / other confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[PRIMARY]** Pliny the Younger, *Epistles* X.96–97 — Latin from a named PD edition; older English is US PD. Reuse the Yeshua case-study source note rather than inventing a second edition — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[PRIMARY]** *Sibylline Oracles* books 4–5 (Nero *redivivus* material) — older English (e.g. Milton S. Terry, 1899, or Charles where PD) and a named PD Greek — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions; modern translations REFERENCE-ONLY — candidate, not yet reviewed
9. **[PRIMARY]** Arabic hadith and related reports on al-Masīḥ ad-Dajjāl — use a named, rights-cleared Arabic text and a PD or openly licensed English only. Many widely used modern translations and sites are in copyright or have mixed terms — **check**; modern translations **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PDF-OK edition is named — candidate, not yet reviewed
10. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Recent commercial Bible translations (NIV, NRSV, etc.) and recent commentaries on Revelation, 2 Thessalonians, and the Johannine epistles — cite, do not copy into a pack PDF
11. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern critical editions still in copyright (recent Nestle-Aland printed text as a commercial book, recent Loebs, recent Sibylline editions) — not for PDFs
