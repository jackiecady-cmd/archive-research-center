# Sources: Slavic case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. PDF flags follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or
CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** *Povest’ vremennykh let* (Primary Chronicle) — named Old East Slavic / Church Slavonic edition (**check** copyright of the standard scholarly text). English: Samuel Hazzard Cross (and related older printings) **check** for PD status. Later translations are REFERENCE-ONLY — PDF: PDF-OK only for a confirmed-PD text and translation — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Helmold of Bosau, *Chronica Slavorum* — Latin PD (19th-c. MGH or similar printings, confirm). Older English if one is PD — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Saxo Grammaticus (Arkona / Slavic temple passages) and Thietmar of Merseburg — Latin from named PD editions (e.g. MGH); older English **check** — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Procopius, notices of the Sclaveni — Greek from a named PD edition; Dewing Loeb (1914–28) volumes may be US PD if pre-1929 (**check** each volume) — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Novgorod birch-bark letters — https://gramoty.ru/ and named editions. Terms and images **check**; often LINK-ONLY for plates — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Alexander Afanasyev (Afanas′ev), *Narodnye russkie skazki* and related collections — 19th-c. Russian US PD; 19th-c. or other pre-1929 English translations **check** — PDF: PDF-OK for named PD printings — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[PRIMARY, object]** Zbruch idol — museum / publication photographs usually LINK-ONLY; modern archaeological discussion often in copyright (REFERENCE-ONLY). Interpretation is debated — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[NOT A SOURCE / FORGERY]** *Book of Veles* (*Velesova kniga*) — modern forgery. May be mentioned in a pack only as a flagged false witness, never as evidence of pre-Christian religion.
9. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Neo-pagan god-lists, recent popular Slavic-mythology handbooks, and copyrighted modern translations of the Chronicle.
