# Case study: Egyptian pantheon (index stub)

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

This is a **stub** for major Egyptian deities, to be expanded later,
under the pantheons index
(`content/language-packs/case-studies/pantheons/README.md`). It is
historical and linguistic. It does not teach temple or magical practice.

## Scope (to expand)

Starting names, not a closed canon: **Set**, **Sekhmet**, **Hathor**,
**Isis**, **Apep** (Apophis), and, as the dossier grows, Osiris, Horus,
Ra / Re, Thoth, Anubis, Nephthys, Bastet, Ptah, Amun, Maat, and others.

Primary corpora rather than modern retellings:

- Pyramid Texts
- Coffin Texts
- Book of the Dead (and related underworld books, later)
- Other ritual and literary compositions as packs are added

Source routes: the **Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae**
(https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/; licence still **check**),
museum and papyrus collections already in the source catalog, and
rights-cleared older printings.

**Edition status (important):**

- **E. A. Wallis Budge** (d. 1934): many **pre-1929** titles are US PD
  and may be used with a quality warning (often outdated). Later titles
  need a date check. Already noted in the source catalog.
- **R. O. Faulkner** (d. 1982): Pyramid Texts, Coffin Texts, and Book of
  the Dead translations are **in copyright — REFERENCE-ONLY**. They must
  not go into PDFs.
- Later translations and grammars (Allen, Hornung, and others) are
  likewise REFERENCE-ONLY unless a specific work is PD or openly licensed.

## Method

- Use Egyptian names and periods (Old Kingdom PT, Middle Kingdom CT, New
  Kingdom BD, later temples) instead of one timeless “Egyptian myth.”
- Set, Sekhmet, and Apep especially attract modern occult readings; those
  are modern perspectives, not evidence for a Pyramid Text spell.
- Coptic Christian texts are not this case study (they may belong with
  Yeshua or a later Coptic dossier).
