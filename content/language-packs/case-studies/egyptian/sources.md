# Sources: Egyptian pantheon case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae — https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/ (live). Licence page not cleared from this environment — **check** — PDF: not cleared until terms are read — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Pyramid Texts — Egyptian from a named edition. Kurt Sethe’s early-20th-c. *Die altaegyptischen Pyramidentexte* (from 1908) may be US PD for pre-1929 volumes (**check**). Faulkner, *The Ancient Egyptian Pyramid Texts*, is **REFERENCE-ONLY**. James P. Allen and later editions are **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Coffin Texts — Adriaan de Buck, *The Egyptian Coffin Texts* (1935–1961), is not a pre-1929 US-PD source. Faulkner’s English is **REFERENCE-ONLY**. A PDF-OK Egyptian text must be named before a pack PDF is planned — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Book of the Dead — Édouard Naville (1886) and many **pre-1929 Budge** printings are US PD, with a quality warning on Budge. Later Budge titles need a date check. Faulkner’s *Book of the Dead* is **REFERENCE-ONLY** — PDF: PD printings only, after a title-level date check — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Griffith Institute, IFAO, museum collections (Egyptian Museum resources, Petrie, Global Egyptian Museum, SMB Ägyptisches Museum, Louvre, BM, Met OA) — images often LINK-ONLY; publications mixed. See the regional catalog — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Faulkner (all major translation volumes), Allen, Hornung, recent commercial Book of the Dead translations, and modern devotional or occult Egyptian manuals — not for PDFs.
