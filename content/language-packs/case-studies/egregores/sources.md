# Sources: Egregores case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

## A. Philology (ancient witnesses)

1. **[PRIMARY]** R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch* (Oxford, 1912) — US PD English, with Greek of the Akhmim / Panopolitanus fragment and discussion of Syncellus. Also his *Apocrypha and Pseudepigrapha of the Old Testament* (1913) where that volume is PD. Internet Archive / Wikisource — PDF: PDF-OK for the 1912/1913 work — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Greek 1 Enoch, Codex Panopolitanus (Akhmim fragment) and Syncellus excerpts — name the PD Greek printing used (often Charles 1912 or an earlier edition such as Lods). Confirm the exact edition when the pack opens — PDF: PDF-OK for a confirmed-PD Greek — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Westminster Leningrad Codex / Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible, Aramaic of Daniel 4:13, 17, 23 — https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb — WLC text PD; morphology CC BY 4.0 — PDF: PDF-OK (credit OSHB if morphology is used) — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Qumran Enoch fragments (4QEn / 4Q201–202 and related). Photographs: Leon Levy DSS Digital Library, https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/ — images typically LINK-ONLY. Modern editions (J. T. Milik, *The Books of Enoch*, 1976; later DJD volumes) are in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Ge‘ez 1 Enoch — Charles 1912 English is PDF-OK as a historical translation. A named PD Ethiopic text (Charles or Flemming, if confirmed PD) may support a later pack. Recent Ethiopic editions and modern English (Nickelsburg, VanderKam, Knibb’s later work as applicable) are **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed

## B. Later occult reception (not evidence for section A)

6. **[PRIMARY, reception]** Éliphas Lévi (Alphonse Louis Constant, d. 1875), including *Dogme et Rituel de la Haute Magie* (1854–1856) — French is US PD. Older English (e.g. A. E. Waite’s late-19th-c. versions) may be US PD if the printing is pre-1929; confirm the edition and date before a PDF. Later reprints and annotated editions are **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[SECONDARY, reception]** Golden Dawn, Martinist, and modern “group mind” / thought-form discussions of the egregore — cite as practice history. Most 20th- and 21st-c. books are in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY**, not for PDFs — candidate, not yet reviewed

8. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Recent commentaries on 1 Enoch and Daniel (Nickelsburg, VanderKam, Collins, etc.) — cite, do not copy into a pack PDF.
