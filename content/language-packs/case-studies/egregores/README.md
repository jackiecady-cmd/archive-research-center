# Case study: Egregores

## Scope

A non-biased historical and linguistic dossier on the word and the idea
later called “egregore.” This case study has two layers that must stay
apart. Later occult use is not evidence for what a Hellenistic Jewish or
Aramaic verse says, and the ancient “watchers” are not evidence for a
nineteenth-century “group mind.”

### A. Attested philology (primary)

- Greek ἐγρήγοροι (*egrēgoroi*, “watchers,” from ἐγείρω / ἐγρήγορα, “to
  be awake, to watch”) in the Greek Book of Enoch (1 Enoch): the Codex
  Panopolitanus / Akhmim fragment, and the excerpts preserved by George
  Syncellus.
- Aramaic עִיר / עִירִין (*ʿir* / *ʿirin*, “watcher(s)”) in Daniel 4:13,
  17, 23 (Aramaic portion of Daniel), and in the Qumran Enoch fragments
  (4QEn / 4Q201–202 and related 1 Enoch copies).
- Ge‘ez 1 Enoch, the only complete ancient version of the book, as the
  witness through which most of 1 Enoch is known. Ethiopic wording is a
  translation tradition, not a licence to read later occult senses back
  into it.

Date every witness. Do not collapse Daniel, Qumran Aramaic, the Akhmim
Greek, Syncellus, and the Ge‘ez book into one “Enochic egregore.”

### B. Later occult reinterpretation (reception only)

- Éliphas Lévi and nineteenth-century French esotericism, where *égrégore*
  is re-used in an occult sense that is not the Enochic “watcher.”
- Later Golden Dawn, Martinist, and modern “group mind” or thought-form
  usage (including popular occult writing). These are logged as reception
  and practice context only.

Out of scope until separately commissioned: systematic angelology as
theology, confessional demonology, and practical instructions for “working
with egregores.”

## Method

- Academic consensus and minority positions are labelled as such.
- Section A and section B never share a reliability claim. A pack on Lévi
  does not raise the score of a pack on Daniel, or the other way around.
- Every pack names its edition, licence, and the date of the witness.
- Copyrighted modern editions and translations are REFERENCE-ONLY and will
  not appear in PDFs.
