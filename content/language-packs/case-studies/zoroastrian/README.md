# Case study: Zoroastrian traditions

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack

## Scope

- **Avestan** texts: the Gāthās, and selected Yašts, from a named
  edition. Nineteenth-century Sacred Books of the East translations
  (Darmesteter, Mills) and Geldner’s Avesta may be US PD if the printing
  is pre-1929 — **check**. Later editions (e.g. many post-war text and
  translation series) are often REFERENCE-ONLY.
- **Pahlavi** literature (for example the *Bundahišn*, *Dēnkard*, and
  the *Ardā Wirāz Nāmag* / Arda Viraf Namag), from a named PD or open
  edition if one exists; otherwise REFERENCE-ONLY until cleared. West
  and related SBE / 19th-c. printings often US PD — **check**.
- The **Vendidad** (Vīdēvdād), including its *daēva* lists, as a primary
  witness for the Iranian evaluation of *daēva* (see the inversion note
  below). Darmesteter SBE English is typically US PD, with a quality
  warning.
- Figures and concepts: Ahura Mazdā, the Amesha Spentas, Anāhitā,
  Mithra, and **Angra Mainyu / Ahriman**.

## The *daeva* / *deva* inversion

In Old Iranian, *daēva* (Avestan) / later *dēw* is a **demonic** or
hostile being. In Old Indic, *deva* is a **god**. The two words are
cognate; the religious evaluation is inverted. Packs must state this
as a historical-linguistic fact about the two traditions, and must
**not** use it to collapse Vedic deities into Avestan demons, or
Avestan *daēvas* into “the Hindu gods.” Cross-link
[`../hindu/`](../hindu/) for the Indic side, without merging the
dossiers.

## Ahriman and the adversary lineage

Claims that **Angra Mainyu / Ahriman** influenced later Jewish or
Christian adversary traditions (the *śāṭān* / *ha-satan* lineage) are a
**scholarly debate, not a settled derivation**. They belong with the
adversary lineage in [`../satan-lucifer/`](../satan-lucifer/), tagged as
a flagged comparative hypothesis, and **must not** be transferred to
the morning-star / *hēlēl* / *lucifer* lineage, or written as if Job
already “meant Ahriman.” See the style rule in that case study: the
two figures are not interchangeable.
