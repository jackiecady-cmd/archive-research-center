# Case study: Lilith

Umbrella index only. Packs live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. `sumerian_lilith` is a **draft** (folder
created 11 November 2026; no READY file). Later packs named below
remain **planned**; those folders have not been created.

Historical and linguistic only. **No ritual, worship, or expulsion
instruction.** Respect later devotion and living practitioners; keep
that honor in the modern layer, not as evidence for what a Sumerian
or Akkadian text says. Linked from [`../mesopotamian/`](../mesopotamian/),
[`../demonization/`](../demonization/), [`../judaism/`](../judaism/),
and [`../pantheons/`](../pantheons/). Shared slugs are one top-level
folder each.

## Scope

Four layers, kept apart:

1. **Sumerian / Mesopotamian** — *líl*, *ki-sikil lil₂-la₂* in
   *Gilgamesh, Enkidu and the Netherworld* (the ḫalub-tree episode);
   Akkadian *lilû / lilītu / ardat-lilî* in Utukkū Lemnūtu; the
   Sumerian King List line on Gilgamesh’s father (*lil₂-la₂*, flagged
   ⚠️ UNCERTAIN); the Burney Relief (identification contested;
   image LINK-ONLY). This is the first packed layer
   (`sumerian_lilith`).
2. **Talmudic / Jewish late antique** — Isaiah 34:14 (*lîlît*);
   b. Shabbat 151b, Niddah 24b, Eruvin 100b, Bava Batra 73a;
   Babylonian Aramaic incantation bowls (Montgomery 1913 PD; later
   corpora REFERENCE-ONLY).
3. **Medieval** — Alphabet of Ben Sira (the “first wife” narrative);
   Zohar and related kabbalistic notices. Not biblical, not Sumerian.
4. **Modern reclamation** — feminist midrash and occult or Pagan
   honor of Lilith, treated respectfully as a **modern perspective**,
   not as ancient evidence. Modern offerings belong here.

The folk etymology Lilith < *laylâ* (“night”) is ⚠️ UNCERTAIN and,
as historical etymology, likely false; the usual account starts from
Sumerian *líl* (wind, breath, spirit).

Offerings are documented only where sources attest them. Mesopotamian
and late antique Jewish sources in the first pack are apotropaic
(expulsion, bowls, amulets), not a devotional offering list. Human
sacrifice is not attested in the Lilītu / Lilith sources of that pack.

## Method

- Date the witness, the language, and the edition separately. Do not
  collapse a Sumerian tree-maiden, an Akkadian incantation class,
  Isaiah’s *lîlît*, a Talmudic demon, a medieval first wife, and a
  modern goddess into one being.
- Academic disagreement is flagged, not resolved by preference.
- Copyrighted modern editions (ETCSL English, Kramer 1938, Geller
  2016, Farber 2014, Soncino, Matt, Plaskow’s wording, and the like)
  are REFERENCE-ONLY.
- No spell-work, no “how to work with Lilith.”
