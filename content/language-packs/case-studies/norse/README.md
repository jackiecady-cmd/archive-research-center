# Case study: Norse

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

Part of the pantheons index. Historical and linguistic only. **No ritual
instruction**, including no seiðr how-to.

## Scope

- **Poetic Edda**, especially *Völuspá*, from the Codex Regius (GKS 2365
  4to) and related manuscript witnesses. Do not collapse poem, prose
  frame, and later interpretation.
- **Prose Edda** (Snorri Sturluson), as a 13th-century learned work, not
  as a timeless pagan catechism. Christian and skaldic framing stays
  labelled.
- **Sagas**, including the Icelandic Saga Database as a finding aid.
  Licence is **per translation**; many older English versions are PD,
  modern ones often are not.
- **Runic inscriptions**, via the Scandinavian Runic-text Database
  (Rundata / Samnordisk runtextdatabas). Licence **check**. These are
  primary epigraphic texts, not illustrations of Eddic myth unless a
  named inscription actually supports the claim.
- **Seiðr in historical narrative**, beginning with *Eiríks saga rauða*
  (the Greenland *völva* episode). This is a textual and historical
  dossier, not instruction in practice.

Preferred PD English starting points: Henry Adams Bellows, *The Poetic
Edda* (1923, US PD); Arthur Gilchrist Brodeur, *The Prose Edda* (1916,
US PD). Later translations (Orchard, Larrington, Byock, Faulkes, etc.)
are REFERENCE-ONLY.

## Method

- Keep Old Norse verse, Snorri’s prose, saga narrative, and runic
  evidence in separate notes even when they share a divine name.
- Modern Ásatrú and related devotion is a modern perspective, not
  evidence for the Codex Regius.
