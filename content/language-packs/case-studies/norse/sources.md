# Sources: Norse case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. PDF flags follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or
CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Poetic Edda / Codex Regius (GKS 2365 4to) — named Old Norse edition (pre-1929 printings of the poems are often US PD; later standard editions **check**). Manuscript images follow the holding library’s terms, usually LINK-ONLY — PDF: PDF-OK for a confirmed-PD Old Norse text — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Henry Adams Bellows, *The Poetic Edda* (New York, 1923) — US PD English — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Prose Edda — named Old Norse edition (**check**); English: Arthur Gilchrist Brodeur, *The Prose Edda* (1916), US PD — PDF: Brodeur PDF-OK; Old Norse only if the edition is cleared — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** *Eiríks saga rauða* (seiðr / *völva* episode) — named Old Norse edition; older PD English if one exists, otherwise a later translation is REFERENCE-ONLY — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Icelandic Saga Database — finding aid for saga texts and translations. **Licence per item**; do not treat the site as a blanket PDF-OK corpus — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Scandinavian Runic-text Database (Rundata / Samnordisk runtextdatabas) — inscriptions. Terms **check** — PDF: not cleared until the licence is read; many entries are scholarly editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Recent translations and commentaries (Faulkes, Larrington, Orchard, Byock, and others) and modern Ásatrú or seiðr manuals — cite, do not copy into a pack PDF.
