# Sources: Mesopotamian pantheon case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** CDLI — https://cdli.earth/ — catalogue, transliterations, and some translations. Text “may be freely copied, aggregated and re-used according to common and fair academic practice,” with credit to CDLI; images are often non-commercial or permission-required — PDF: text generally PDF-OK with attribution; images REFERENCE-ONLY / permission required — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** ORACC — https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/ — annotated corpora, CC BY-SA 3.0 (share-alike on adaptations) — PDF: PDF-OK as CC-BY-SA, with attribution — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** ETCSL — https://etcsl.orinst.ox.ac.uk/ — editors’ copyright 1998–2006. Use as a scholarly orientation and, later, as a **reference gold standard for evaluating AI translations**; do not put ETCSL wording in pack PDFs unless permission is granted — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** ISAC / Oriental Institute publications and IDB — https://isac.uchicago.edu/ ; https://isac-idb.uchicago.edu/ — many older OIP / AS volumes are free to read and often PD or openly posted; confirm each title — PDF: mixed; PDF-OK for confirmed PD or CC volumes — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Penn Museum, Vorderasiatisches Museum / SMB, British Museum, Louvre, and related tablet holders — object pages often LINK-ONLY for images; texts frequently also in CDLI. Licence per record — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern English literary translations (Dalley, Foster, Jacobsen, and later Inanna popular versions) — cite, do not copy into a pack PDF.
