# Sources: Celtic case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below still need a per-edition check when a
pack is opened. PDF flags follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or
CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** CELT (Corpus of Electronic Texts), University College Cork — https://celt.ucc.ie/ — Irish and some Welsh and Latin texts, often with English. **Licence is per document and is a check** (some editorial files may be NC or rights-reserved) — PDF: not cleared until the file header is read — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** *Cath Maige Tuired* — name a PD Irish edition if possible (e.g. Stokes, late 19th / early 20th c., **check** the printing) and/or a CELT file whose licence allows reuse — PDF: PDF-OK only for a cleared edition — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** *Lebor Gabála Érenn* and *Táin Bó Cúailnge* — same rule: named edition, CELT header, or confirmed-PD printing (Stokes, Windisch, Strachan, and others, date-checked) — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Middle Welsh Mabinogi — named Welsh edition (**check** copyright; later scholarly editions are often still in copyright). English: Lady Charlotte Guest, *The Mabinogion* (1838–49 and later 19th-c. printings), US PD — PDF: Guest PDF-OK; Welsh only if the edition is cleared — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Book of Taliesin — named Middle Welsh edition; Guest and other pre-1929 English only after a title check — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Gaulish inscriptions: Larzac, Chamalières, and RIG (*Recueil des inscriptions gauloises*). Modern RIG volumes may be in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PDF-OK text or a rights-cleared diplomatic transcription is named. Images of the objects: usually LINK-ONLY — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[PRIMARY]** Caesar, *Bellum Gallicum* (druid and Gallic-religion passages) and Pliny, *Naturalis Historia* (druids, mistletoe, etc.) — PD Latin and older English (e.g. 19th-c. Bohn / pre-1930 Loeb if confirmed PD) — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD editions — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[REFERENCE]** eDIL (electronic Dictionary of the Irish Language) and GPC (Geiriadur Prifysgol Cymru) — cite entries; do not dump recent dictionary text into PDFs. Terms **check**.
9. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern translations and pagan or devotional Celtic handbooks — cite as reception, not as evidence, and not for PDFs.
