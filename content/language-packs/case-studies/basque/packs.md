# Planned packs — Basque

All paths are top-level under `content/language-packs/`. None of these
folders has been created. Status of every row: **planned**.

| Slug | Path | Focus | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `aquitanian_inscriptions` | `/workspace/language-packs/aquitanian_inscriptions/` | Aquitanian inscriptions and divine or personal names, from a named, rights-cleared corpus. Modern editions often REFERENCE-ONLY until cleared. Flag uncertainty | planned |
| `basque_mari_folklore` | `/workspace/language-packs/basque_mari_folklore/` | Mari (and closely related figures such as Sugaar) in dated folklore collections. PD 19th-c. printings if they exist and are sound; later collections often REFERENCE-ONLY. Not a reconstructed cult handbook | planned |

| `french_de_lancre_tableau` | `/workspace/language-packs/french_de_lancre_tableau/` | Pierre de Lancre, *Tableau de l’inconstance des mauvais anges* (1612, PD; Gallica). Historical/textual only; no ritual how-to | planned |
| `basque_etxepare_1545` | `/workspace/language-packs/basque_etxepare_1545/` | Bernard Etxepare, *Linguae Vasconum Primitiae* (1545, PD), as an early Basque language witness | planned |

The Mari folklore pack should also note **Sugaar / Sugoi**, **Basajaun**,
**lamiak**, and **akelarre** where the dated collection actually uses
those names. Barandiaran remains REFERENCE-ONLY pending a copyright
check and is not a first-wave PDF source.

Possible later packs: a language-history note if a rights-cleared
Aquitanian / Basque grammatical source is named; a Barandiaran pack only
if a licensed or confirmed-PD item is identified.
