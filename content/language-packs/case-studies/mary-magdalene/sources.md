# Sources: Mary Magdalene

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Reliability scores below are **provisional collection tiers**
(1–5), not pack scores; rescore with the seven-criterion rubric when a
pack opens. PDF flags: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs.

Compiled 10 November 2026 (America/Chicago). Tags: canonical / early
extra-canonical / patristic conflation / liturgical revision / medieval
legend / modern reception / forgery.

8. **[PRIMARY] [patristic conflation]** Gregory the Great, *Homilia in Evangelia* 33, 591 — Latin in Migne PL or another pre-1929 printing may be PDF-OK (**check** the printing). English: name a PD translation if one exists; modern English REFERENCE-ONLY. This is the standard Western source for conflating Mary Magdalene with Mary of Bethany and the anonymous woman of Luke 7 — License: PL / pre-1929 PD where confirmed — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD printings — Reliability (provisional): 4 as a dated homily and as evidence of a 6th-c. conflation, **not** as evidence of first-century identity — candidate, not yet reviewed

9. **[SECONDARY] [liturgical revision]** General Roman Calendar revision, 1969 — distinguishes Mary Magdalene in the Roman liturgy from the Lukan “sinner” and from Mary of Bethany. Official modern liturgical books and commentary are typically copyrighted — License: Vatican / ICEL and related holders, usually all-rights-reserved — PDF: **REFERENCE-ONLY** or LINK-ONLY; cite, do not paste; **no Vatican partnership claim** — Reliability (provisional): 4 as a dated liturgical fact once the official document is cited; 2 if described only from secondary news — candidate, not yet reviewed

11. **[PRIMARY] [medieval legend]** Jacobus de Voragine, *Legenda aurea* (Golden Legend), Mary Magdalene chapter, and related Provence / Vézelay / Sainte-Baume traditions — Latin and early English (Caxton; other pre-1929 printings) may be PD. The latter is fiction. **Not evidence** for an ancient marriage, child, or bloodline — License: all-rights-reserved — PDF: **REFERENCE-ONLY**; do not quote at length; do not pack as primary sources — Reliability (provisional): not scored as ancient evidence (modern non-evidence). Not an ancient witness — License: irrelevant for packing; do not treat as primary — PDF: do not pack as a source text; a short labelled note may cite the scholarly discussion (check quotation rights) — Reliability (provisional): 1 as an ancient text (forgery).
