# Sources: Hindu traditions case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Reliability scores are **provisional collection tiers** (1–5).

1. **[PRIMARY]** GRETIL (Göttingen Register of Electronic Texts in Indian Languages) — https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/ — typically “free for scholarly use,” not automatically CC — License: **check** the current project statement and the file header — PDF: check; not assumed PDF-OK — Reliability (provisional): 4 as a scholarly text collection, pending licence — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** Rigveda — named Sanskrit edition or GRETIL file; English: Ralph T. H. Griffith (late 19th c.), US PD, quality warning — License: Griffith PD; Sanskrit per edition — PDF: Griffith PDF-OK; Sanskrit only if cleared — Reliability (provisional): 4 for a standard Sanskrit text; 3 for Griffith as English — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Bhagavad Gītā — named Sanskrit; 19th-c. English (Thomson, Telang in SBE, or similar) often US PD (**check** the title) — License: per edition — PDF: PDF-OK for confirmed-PD printings; recent translations REFERENCE-ONLY — Reliability (provisional): 4 for a named standard Sanskrit; 3 for dated PD English — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Devī Māhātmya (*Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa*), including Kālī / Durgā passages that stand in the named text; *Bhāgavata Purāṇa* as related purāṇic context — named Sanskrit; PD English if identified — License: **check** — PDF: not cleared until the edition is named — Reliability (provisional): unscored until a text is named; likely 4 for a standard Sanskrit edition — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** Mahābhārata — English: Kisari Mohan Ganguli (1883–1896), US PD. Sanskrit: a named edition; BORI critical edition **licensing check** (not assumed PD or CC). Rāmāyaṇa: same pattern (older PD English such as Griffith or Dutt **check**; Baroda / other critical editions licensing check) — License: Ganguli PD; critical editions check — PDF: Ganguli PDF-OK; Sanskrit only if the named edition is cleared — Reliability (provisional): 3 for Ganguli as dated English; 4 for a standard Sanskrit text once licence is clear — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY]** Upaniṣads — English: Max Müller, *Sacred Books of the East* (vols. 1 and 15, 1879/1884), US PD, quality warning. Sanskrit from a named PD or open edition — License: Müller SBE PD; Sanskrit per edition — PDF: Müller PDF-OK; Sanskrit if cleared — Reliability (provisional): 3 for Müller as dated English; 4 for a standard Sanskrit text — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[SECONDARY]** Muktabodha Digital Library, SARIT, Cologne Digital Sanskrit Dictionaries — License: **check** per project; lexica often LINK-ONLY / REFERENCE-ONLY for dumps — PDF: not cleared by default — Reliability (provisional): 3–4 as finding aids — candidate, not yet reviewed
