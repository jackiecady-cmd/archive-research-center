# Case study: Hecate

Umbrella index only. Packs live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. `greek_hecate_theogony` is a **draft**
(folder created 10 November 2026; no READY file). The hymn and PGM packs
remain **planned**; those folders have not been created.

## Scope
A non-biased historical and linguistic dossier on Hecate (Greek Ἑκάτη,
*Hekatē*; Latin *Hecate* / *Trivia*), from the earliest literary witnesses
through late antique magical texts. In scope:

- Hesiod, *Theogony* 411–452 — the earliest extended account: honours in
  earth, sea, and sky; κουροτρόφος. The “triple Hecate” of later art and
  magic is **not** in this passage and must not be read back into it.
- *Homeric Hymn to Demeter* — Hecate hears Persephone and becomes her
  πρόπολος (attendant / guide).
- Orphic Hymn 1 (to Hecate), from a rights-cleared Greek text; Thomas
  Taylor 1792 is US PD English. Athanassakis and other modern English
  translations are REFERENCE-ONLY.
- Chaldean Oracles: fragmentary, late antique, and textually difficult.
  Modern editions (des Places, Majercik, etc.) are in copyright —
  REFERENCE-ONLY until a PD Greek text is identified and the reconstruction
  problems are flagged.
- PGM Hecate–Selene–Artemis hymns (especially in PGM IV). Greek from
  Preisendanz vol. 1 (1928) is US PD, with an EU/UK copyright warning.
  **Betz’s English is REFERENCE-ONLY and must not be copied into a PDF.**
- Defixiones / curse tablets that name Hecate, from named publications
  (licence per item).
- Epithets: see `epithets.md`. Well-attested names only, with a source; later
  or overlapping titles (for example Βριμώ, also used of Persephone) are
  flagged ⚠️ UNCERTAIN.
- Roman *Trivia* and Latin literary identifications (Virgil, Horace, and
  others), kept labelled as Roman reception, not as Archaic Greek data.

Later grimoires, Wiccan, and modern occult Hecate material may be logged as
practice context when needed. They are not evidence for what Hesiod or a
PGM papyrus says.

## Method
- Date every witness and do not collapse Archaic, Classical, Hellenistic,
  Roman, and late-antique layers.
- Keep Greek religion, Roman reception, and magical papyri in separate
  notes even when they share an epithet.
- Academic disagreement (for example how far Thessalian Ἐνοδία is
  independent of Hecate) is flagged, not resolved by preference.
