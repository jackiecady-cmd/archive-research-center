# Hecate epithets (stub)

Filled only where a primary attestation can be named with some confidence.
“First attestation” dates are provisional and must be checked against the
edition when a pack is opened. Overlap with Artemis, Selene, Persephone, or
an independent local goddess is flagged ⚠️ UNCERTAIN, not smoothed over.

## Deliberately not filled yet
Titles such as Βριμώ (*Brimo*), Νυκτιπόλος (*Nyktipolos*), Ἄγγελος
(*Angelos*), and many PGM string-epithets need a line-by-line pass. Several
of these are shared with Persephone, Demeter, Artemis, or Selene, or are
unique to one papyrus. They will be added with citations or left out.

Modern occult lists of “Hecate’s epithets” are not a source for this table.
