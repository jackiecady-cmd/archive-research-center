# Sources: Hecate case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. PDF flags follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA
go into PDFs.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Hesiod, *Theogony* 411–452 — Greek in a named edition (Perseus / a PD Teubner or Oxford text, licence to confirm); English: Hugh G. Evelyn-White, Loeb 1914, US PD (Wikisource / Internet Archive) — PDF: PDF-OK for the 1914 English and for a confirmed-PD Greek — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** *Homeric Hymn to Demeter* (Hecate at, especially, 19–25 and 438–440) — same Evelyn-White 1914 PD English; PD Greek to be named — PDF: PDF-OK as above — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** Orphic Hymn 1 (to Hecate) — Greek from a pre-1929 edition (Quandt 1955 is later and likely still in copyright in some jurisdictions — check before use). English: Thomas Taylor, *The Mystical Hymns of Orpheus*, 1792, US PD — PDF: Taylor PDF-OK; modern English (Athanassakis, etc.) **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** Preisendanz, *Papyri Graecae Magicae*, vol. 1 (Leipzig, 1928) — Greek (and 1928 German). US PD from 1 January 2024; EU/UK generally in copyright until end of 2038 ⚠️. Vol. 2 (1931) is US PD only from 1 January 2027 — PDF: vol. 1 US PDF-OK with jurisdiction warning; vol. 2 not yet — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Hans Dieter Betz (ed.), *The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation, Including the Demotic Spells* (University of Chicago Press, 1986/1992) — in copyright. Cite, do not copy into a pack PDF.
6. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Henrichs’s second edition of Preisendanz (Teubner, 1973–74) — in copyright.
7. **[PRIMARY]** Individual PGM papyri (e.g. PGM IV, BnF) via museum pages or papyri.info — licence per item, images often restricted — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[PRIMARY]** Defixiones naming Hecate — Audollent and later corpora; licence and language per tablet — candidate, not yet reviewed
9. **[PRIMARY]** Latin *Trivia*: Virgil, Horace, and related passages — PD Latin and older English — candidate, not yet reviewed
10. **[SECONDARY / REFERENCE-ONLY until a PD Greek is named]** Chaldean Oracles — modern editions (des Places, Majercik) are in copyright and the text is reconstructive. Flag uncertainties heavily.
11. **[REFERENCE]** LSJ and related lexica for epithets; inscription corpora (e.g. material from Lagina) — cite by entry. Recent dictionaries may be in copyright; quote sparingly under fair use, do not dump entries into PDFs.
12. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern devotional or occult Hecate books and websites — practice context only, never as evidence for an ancient reading.
