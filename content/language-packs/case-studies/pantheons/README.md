# Pantheons index (stub)

This folder is an **index**, not a case study with packs of its own and
not a container for pack folders. Tradition-level case studies live
beside it under `/workspace/language-packs/case-studies/<name>/`. Packs
remain top-level at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`.

The Archive & Research Center is historical and research-only,
all-inclusive, and non-biased. It covers **all pantheons, gods, and
goddesses, including infernal and goetic figures.** There is **no
spell-work instruction.** AI translations, when offered, are for
knowledge. Modern devotional readings — including a “dark divine” view of
infernal figures — are logged as modern perspectives, not as evidence.

This stub can grow into a fuller map. Names below are starting points,
not a closed canon.

## Case studies already opened

- [`hecate/`](../hecate/) — Greek Hecate, hymns, epithets, PGM; part of
  the earlier goddess series
- [`mesopotamian/`](../mesopotamian/) — Enki/Ea, Enlil, Inanna/Ishtar;
  includes the priority pack `sumerian_inanna_descent`
- [`egyptian/`](../egyptian/) — stub for Set, Sekhmet, Hathor, Isis,
  Apep, and others; Pyramid / Coffin Texts and Book of the Dead
- [`demonization/`](../demonization/) — how gods were reclassified as
  demons; *Grand Grimoire* / “Red Dragon” as entry point (dating
  flagged); *shedim*, *daimonia*, Astarte→Astaroth, Baal Zebub, Weyer
- [`goetia/`](../goetia/) — Ars Goetia and antecedents (Weyer 1577,
  *Testament of Solomon*, Solomonic textual lineage, 17th-c. MSS);
  72-spirit historical index stub; no ritual instruction
- [`celtic/`](../celtic/) — Irish (including the Morrígan and the Tuatha
  Dé), Welsh Mabinogi, Gaulish inscriptions, classical notices of druids
- [`norse/`](../norse/) — Poetic and Prose Edda, *Völuspá*, sagas, runic
  inscriptions, historical seiðr notices
- [`siberian/`](../siberian/) — Evenki, Sakha Olonkho, Buryat, Khanty /
  Mansi, Nenets; Orkhon inscriptions and Tengrism; Jesup ethnography.
  Flag ethnographer bias and living-tradition sensitivity
- [`alpine/`](../alpine/) — Perchta, Krampus, Wild Hunt, Raetic
  inscriptions, Valais 1428, *Malleus Maleficarum*, Grimm
- [`slavic/`](../slavic/) — Perun, Veles, Mokosh, later folklore
  (Morana, Baba Yaga), Chronicle and Latin ethnography; *Book of Veles*
  flagged as a forgery
- [`satan-lucifer/`](../satan-lucifer/) — two distinct figures (adversary
  lineage and morning-star lineage) plus a dated conflation section;
  the two names are not used interchangeably
- [`gnostic/`](../gnostic/) — Nag Hammadi, *Pistis Sophia*, Mandaean
  *Ginza*, heresiological reports
- [`judaism/`](../judaism/) — Jewish primary and interpretive texts
  (Tanakh, Targums, *Sefer Yetzirah*, Ben Sira); not a Yeshua duplicate
- [`hindu/`](../hindu/) — Vedic, epic, and selected purāṇic Sanskrit
  texts; no ritual instruction
- [`zoroastrian/`](../zoroastrian/) — Avestan and Pahlavi sources;
  *daeva*/*deva* inversion; Ahriman linked to the adversary lineage only
  as a flagged scholarly debate
- [`basque/`](../basque/) — Aquitanian inscriptions, Mari and related
  folklore, early-modern trial records

## Method (shared)

- Attested philology and dated sources first; later reception and modern
  devotion labelled as such.
- Infernal and goetic figures belong in the research scope; their history
  of **demotion** is a research question (`demonization/`, `goetia/`),
  not a reason to exclude them or to treat a grimoire as a Bronze Age
  witness.
- No evocation, no spell-work, no “how to work with” a deity or spirit.
