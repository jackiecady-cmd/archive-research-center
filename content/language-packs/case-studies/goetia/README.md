# Case study: Goetia

Umbrella index only. Packs will live at `/workspace/language-packs/<slug>/`
and are listed in `packs.md`. Status of those packs: **planned**. No pack
folders have been created yet. No translations have been started.

This is a **historical and textual** dossier on the Goetia and its
antecedents, as far back as the sources will go. It is **not** a grimoire
for use. There is **no ritual or spell-work instruction.** Demotion links
to earlier deity names are cross-referenced with [`../demonization/`](../demonization/),
[`../satan-lucifer/`](../satan-lucifer/) (adversary and morning-star
lineages kept distinct), and the pantheons index.

## Scope

- **Ars Goetia**, the first book of the *Lesser Key of Solomon*
  (*Lemegeton*). Seventeenth-century manuscripts, including British
  Library Sloane 2731, Sloane 3825, and Harley 6483 (image licences are
  usually LINK-ONLY; confirm each record).
- Johann Weyer, *Pseudomonarchia Daemonum* (1577, PD) — an earlier Latin
  hierarchy from which many Goetia names and offices derive. Shared pack
  slug with the demonization case study: `latin_pseudomonarchia_weyer`.
- *Testament of Solomon* (Greek; commonly dated somewhere in the
  **1st–5th centuries**, with the date itself debated). Solomonic
  demonology earlier than the early-modern grimoires, not a “Goetia”
  in the *Lemegeton* sense.
- The wider Solomonic magic lineage as **textual history**: the
  *Hygromanteia* (*Magical Treatise of Solomon*) and the *Clavicula
  Salomonis* (Key of Solomon). Later packs only, and only as history of
  texts — not as working manuals.
- The 1904 Mathers / Crowley English *Goetia* (*The Book of the Goetia of
  Solomon the King*). **PD status is a check:** the 1904 printing is
  pre-1929 and is likely US PD; Crowley's death in 1947 means some
  jurisdictions need a date-and-contribution check; later reprints with
  new introductions are REFERENCE-ONLY. Do not treat a modern Weiser (or
  similar) edition as the PDF source.
- A **72-spirit index stub** (`spirits.md`): name, earliest textual
  source, and any attested link to an earlier deity name. Where the link
  is speculative (for example Paimon), flag **⚠️ UNCERTAIN**. Examples
  already noted: Bael ↔ Baal; Astaroth ↔ Astarte / Ashtoreth. This table
  is a historical index, not a calling list.

Out of scope: evocation, circles, tools, “how to work with the 72,” and
any other ritual instruction. Modern devotional or “dark divine” readings
are modern perspectives, not evidence for what Weyer or a Sloane
manuscript says.

## Method

- Work backwards from the early-modern manuscripts; do not read the 1904
  English, or a nineteenth-century *Dragon Rouge*, back into the
  *Testament of Solomon* or into Bronze Age deity names.
- Where a Goetia name and a deity name are etymologically connected, say
  so as a historical hypothesis and cite the earlier source. Where they
  are not, say ⚠️ UNCERTAIN or “no attested basis.”
- Every pack names its edition, licence, and the date of the witness.
- Copyrighted modern editions (including Marathakis on the *Hygromanteia*
  and recent Keys of Solomon) are REFERENCE-ONLY.
