# Sources: Goetia case study

Seed list. Every item is **candidate, not yet reviewed**, unless marked
otherwise. Licences stated below are from the 10 November 2026 source
catalog and still need a per-edition check when a pack is opened. PDF flags
follow the archive rule: only PD, CC-BY, or CC-BY-SA go into PDFs. This
list is for historical study; it is not a ritual bibliography.

1. **[PRIMARY]** Johann Weyer, *Pseudomonarchia Daemonum* (1577) — Latin US PD. Confirm a named printing and any PD English — PDF: PDF-OK — candidate, not yet reviewed
2. **[PRIMARY]** British Library, Sloane 2731, Sloane 3825, Harley 6483 (*Lemegeton* / Ars Goetia witnesses, 17th c.) — catalogue and any digitised images: **LINK-ONLY** until a record’s statement is read. A diplomatic transcription made from a rights-cleared image, or a confirmed-PD printing of the English, may be PDF-OK; the plates are not — candidate, not yet reviewed
3. **[PRIMARY]** *Testament of Solomon* — Greek: McCown, *The Testament of Solomon* (1922) is a candidate US-PD edition (**check**). English: F. C. Conybeare, *Jewish Quarterly Review* 11 (1898), US PD — PDF: PDF-OK if those printings are confirmed — candidate, not yet reviewed
4. **[PRIMARY]** *Hygromanteia* / *Magical Treatise of Solomon* — medieval Greek MSS; modern editions (e.g. Marathakis) are in copyright — **REFERENCE-ONLY** until a PDF-OK Greek is named — candidate, not yet reviewed
5. **[PRIMARY]** *Clavicula Salomonis* (Key of Solomon) — many early-modern Latin, Italian, and French manuscripts and prints. Licence and language **per witness**. Nineteenth- and twentieth-century popular editions need a date check; recent scholarly editions are **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
6. **[PRIMARY, reception, PD check]** S. L. MacGregor Mathers and Aleister Crowley, *The Book of the Goetia of Solomon the King* (1904). Pre-1929 US printing: **likely US PD, confirm**. Crowley’s original contributions: check jurisdiction (US 1904 publication vs. life+70 elsewhere). Later reprints and new introductions: **REFERENCE-ONLY** — candidate, not yet reviewed
7. **[SECONDARY]** *Grand Grimoire* / *Le Dragon Rouge* and de Plancy, *Dictionnaire Infernal* — reception context; see `../demonization/sources.md` — candidate, not yet reviewed
8. **[REFERENCE-ONLY]** Modern occult manuals, contemporary Keys of Solomon, and any edition used as a working grimoire — not for PDFs, and not to be paraphrased as instruction.
