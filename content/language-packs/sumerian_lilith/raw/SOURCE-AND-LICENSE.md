## Used in this pack (PDF-eligible wording)

1. CDLI Literary 000364 composite, P469670 (Q000364), Gilgamesh, Enkidu
   and the Netherworld. Transliteration (ATF/sign values) used with
   attribution under CDLI Terms of Use (https://cdli.earth/terms-of-use,
   consulted 11 November 2026): "Transliterations and translations Text
   in the pages of CDLI may be freely copied, aggregated and re-used
   according to common and fair academic practice" with credit to CDLI
   and https://cdli.earth for considerable reuse. **Images are not used**
   (often NC / permission).
   URL: https://cdli.earth/artifacts/469670
   The typographic transliteration in pack.md is our normalized rendering
   of those CDLI sign readings (sz→š, {gesz}→ĝeš, etc.), checked against
   ePSD2 values.

2. R. Campbell Thompson, *The Devils and Evil Spirits of Babylonia*,
   vol. 1 (London: Luzac, 1903). US and EU/UK public domain (published
   1903; Thompson d. 1941, life+70 ended 2011). Used for Utukkū Lemnūtu
   (then called Utukki Limnuti) bilingual lines and English.
   Source consulted: Internet Archive, devilsevilspirit01thomuoft.

3. J. A. Montgomery, *Aramaic Incantation Texts from Nippur*
   (Philadelphia: University Museum, 1913). US PD (published 1913).
   Source consulted: Internet Archive text of that edition.

4. Isaiah 34:14, Masoretic (Leningrad / standard WLC wording). The
   biblical Hebrew wording is not in copyright. English published
   translation: King James Version (1611/1769), US PD.

5. Sumerian King List, line on Gilgamesh’s father: cited from the
   conventional sign sequence of the Weld-Blundell prism (WB 444) as
   published by S. Langdon, *Oxford Editions of Cuneiform Texts* II,
   *Historical Inscriptions* (1923) — US PD (published 1923). Meaning
   of lil₂-la₂ is flagged UNCERTAIN.
