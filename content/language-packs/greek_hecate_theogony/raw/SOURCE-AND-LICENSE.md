## Texts used

1. Greek and English of Hugh G. Evelyn-White, *Hesiod, the Homeric Hymns
   and Homerica*, Loeb Classical Library (London: William Heinemann;
   New York: Macmillan, 1914). Public domain in the United States
   (published 1914) and in the EU/UK (Evelyn-White died 1924; life+70
   expired at the end of 1994).

2. Digital source: Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University.
   Greek HTML:
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0129:card=404
   Greek XML:
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/xmlchunk?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0129%3Acard%3D404
   English HTML:
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus:text:1999.01.0130:card=404
   English XML:
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/xmlchunk?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0130%3Acard%3D404
   Copyright help (consulted 10 November 2026):
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/help/copyright.jsp
   Perseus states that public-domain texts are available as XML
   downloads. Perseus markup is often also described as CC BY-SA; the
   files in this folder are plain-text extracts of the 1914 PD wording,
   with line numbers from the Perseus TEI, and attribution to
   Evelyn-White 1914 and to Perseus. If a later reuse treats the XML
   encoding itself as a Perseus derivative, honor CC BY-SA for that
   markup.
