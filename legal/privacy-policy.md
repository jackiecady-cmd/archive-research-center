## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:

- create and secure accounts, and keep signed-in sessions working;
- provide concierge guidance, translation stations, forums, and private rooms;
- process payments and complimentary access;
- investigate blocked comp-code redemptions and other security incidents;
- moderate content and enforce the Terms of Use and the Copyright and DMCA Policy;
- respond to copyright notifications, counter-notifications, and other legal requests;
- communicate about the service; and
- improve reliability and fix errors.

We do not use forum, private-room, or translation contents to train public AI models. [IF ANY VENDOR’S TERMS ALLOW THE VENDOR TO TRAIN ON SUBMITTED TEXT, STATE THAT AND NAME THE VENDOR — currently no public vendor is named, and model names stay internal.]

We disclose information only as follows:

- **Service providers** that process data for us, such as [HOSTING PROVIDER], [PAYMENT PROCESSOR], [EMAIL PROVIDER], and [ANY ERROR-LOGGING OR ANALYTICS PROVIDER], under contracts that limit their use of the data.
- **Jacqueline and her active delegate**, for account administration, complimentary-code issuance, and review of comp-code incidents.
- **The member who posted content**, when we notify that member that we have removed or disabled material after a copyright notice (see the Copyright and DMCA Policy and 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)).
- **A copyright complainant**, when we forward a counter-notification as described in 17 U.S.C. § 512(g)(2).
- **Law enforcement or a court**, when we believe disclosure is required by law, including a subpoena under 17 U.S.C. § 512(h) if one is properly issued.
- **A buyer or successor**, if the legal entity that operates the service is transferred, [SUBJECT TO COUNSEL’S ADVICE ON NOTICE].

We do not post VIP invitees’ names as members or supporters unless the invitee has agreed in writing.
