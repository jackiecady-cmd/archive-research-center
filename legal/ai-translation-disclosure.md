## Approved public wording

> The English translations here are AI-generated and peer-reviewed by other AI translators against the cited source editions. The source text is shown unaltered; translations are a separate layer.

Do **not** add model names, provider names, or product names to this sentence or to any other public page, response, or credit line.

## Where it should appear

- On translation workspaces and on any page or API response that returns an English translation layer (already implemented on pull request #4 as `TRANSLATION_DISCLOSURE`).
- In a site footer or similarly durable location once Lexicon wires public policy and footer pages.
- At the top of language-pack `pack.md` files that include an English translation layer (already added by Lexicon, with `disclosure_wording: approved`).

## What it does not say

- It does not claim that a human scholar certified the translation.
- It does not claim copyright in the translation.
- It does not name the systems that produced or reviewed the translation.
- It does not replace credit lines for the **source edition** (for example Evelyn-White 1914, or a named public-domain English Bible).
